You control Bob, a physics-driven ragdoll with no fixed shape or gravity script to save him — every grab, climb, and fall is simulated live, which is exactly why so much of the game is funnier than it means to be.

Levels are open-ended dreamscapes with multiple solutions to each puzzle, and the free Workshop adds thousands of community-made levels on top of the base game. It's best with friends, but the physics chaos holds up solo too.
