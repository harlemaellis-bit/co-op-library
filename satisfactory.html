You are dropped onto planet MASSAGE-2(A-B)b as a Pioneer working for FICSIT Inc., tasked with establishing automated supply chains for an interstellar project of unclear purpose. What starts as a single smelter and a handful of conveyor belts tends to spiral into a continent-spanning industrial complex.

There's no fixed objective beyond FICSIT's ever-expanding project milestones — how you get there is up to you. The game left Early Access in September 2024, then arrived on Xbox Series X|S and PS5 in November 2025 with console-to-console crossplay.
